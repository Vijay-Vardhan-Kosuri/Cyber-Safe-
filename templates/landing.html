The CyberSafe Posture Lifecycle

A disciplined, continuous cycle turning policy expectations into verified technical safeguards.

Step 01

Scope & Model

Platform Super Admins and Org Admins configure multi-tenant organizations, establish user roles, and assign versioned CIS/NIST baselines.

Step 02

Assess & Score

Employees complete interactive response questionnaires. CyberSafe calculates a transparent compliance score excluding non-applicable items.

Step 03

Remediate & Submit

Deficiencies automatically spawn remediation tasks. Assigned staff document corrective actions, upload evidence, and submit for verification.

Step 04

Verify & Report

Security Analysts independently verify evidence, close resolved tickets, update organization dashboards, and generate CSV reports.

Engineering Rigor

Enterprise Cybersecurity Capabilities

Architected with strict multi-tenancy, server-side RBAC, and real-time database analytics.

Strict 4-Tier RBAC

Platform Super Admin, Organization Admin, Security Analyst, and Employee roles enforced at the ORM and view layer. No client-side bypasses.

Multi-Tenant Isolation

Complete tenant separation. Assessments, remediation tasks, and evidence submitted by one organization cannot be queried by another.

Transparent Scoring

Deterministic scoring weighted by priority and category. Never scores N/A controls as failures or unanswered items as compliant.

Remediation State Machine

Structured transitions: Open → In Progress → Submitted for Review → Verified. Reviewers may return tasks with explanations.

Audit & CSV Export

Immutable audit trails recording user logins, role changes, and reviews. Complete CSV exports for compliance auditors and leadership.

MySQL 8 & PyMySQL

Production-ready MySQL 8 database integration with foreign keys, transactional integrity, and automated fallback for local test runs.
