CyberSafe

Personal & Organizational Cybersecurity Management Platform. Benchmarking compliance, coordinating remediation, and safeguarding digital assets through structured posture management.

CIS Controls v8 NIST CSF 2.0 ISO 27001 Aligned
Important Cybersecurity Posture Notice CyberSafe evaluates cybersecurity posture via authorized checklist responses, self-attestation, and submitted evidence. It does not perform active network penetration, execute invasive packet sniffing, or detect real-time malware. A completed assessment reflects documented practices and does not independently guarantee security immunity.
